Compliance Requirements

KYC / AML

All authorized persons must pass individual KYC verification. OSN conducts automated AML screening against global sanctions lists.

KYB (Know Your Business)

Corporate entity verification through business registration, UBO identification, and beneficial ownership structure review.

Bank Account Verification

Bank account must be in the name of the registered business entity. Micro-deposit verification may be required.

Wallet Whitelisting

Crypto wallets must be proven via cryptographic signature. Only whitelisted wallets can initiate off-ramp transfers.
